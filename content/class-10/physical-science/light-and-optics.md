# Light: Reflection, Refraction and Lens Optics

## 1. Principles of Geometric Optics
Geometric optics models light propagation in terms of rays. Light travels along rectilinear paths in homogeneous isotropic media.

## 2. Laws of Reflection & Refraction

### Snell's Law of Refraction:
When a ray of light passes from medium 1 (refractive index $n_1$) to medium 2 (refractive index $n_2$):

```text
n₁ · sin(θ₁) = n₂ · sin(θ₂)
```

Where:
* $\theta_1$ is the angle of incidence
* $\theta_2$ is the angle of refraction

## 3. Thin Lens Formula & Magnification
For spherical thin lenses in paraxial approximation:

```text
1/v - 1/u = 1/f
m = v/u = hᵢ / hₒ
```

* $u$ = Object distance from optical centre (negative in Cartesian sign convention)
* $v$ = Image distance
* $f$ = Focal length (positive for convex, negative for concave)
* $m$ = Linear magnification

> [!NOTE]
> Cartesian sign convention: All distances measured in the direction of incident light are positive. Distances opposite to incident light are negative.
