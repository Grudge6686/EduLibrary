# Motion, Force and Laws of Dynamics

## 1. Kinematics Fundamentals
Kinematics describes the motion of points, bodies, and systems of bodies without considering the forces that cause them to move.

### Primary Kinematic Variables:
* **Displacement ($s$):** Vector quantity indicating change in position.
* **Velocity ($v$):** Rate of change of displacement with respect to time ($v = \frac{ds}{dt}$).
* **Acceleration ($a$):** Rate of change of velocity ($a = \frac{dv}{dt}$).

## 2. Equations of Uniformly Accelerated Motion
For an object moving in a straight line with constant acceleration $a$:

```text
v = u + at
s = ut + ½ at²
v² = u² + 2as
s = ½ (u + v)t
```

Where:
* $u$ = Initial velocity (m/s)
* $v$ = Final velocity (m/s)
* $a$ = Acceleration (m/s²)
* $t$ = Time elapsed (s)
* $s$ = Total displacement (m)

## 3. Newton's Laws of Motion

> [!NOTE]
> **First Law (Inertia):** Every body continues in its state of rest, or of uniform motion in a straight line, unless compelled to change that state by forces impressed upon it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Second Law (Momentum & Force):** The rate of change of momentum of a body is directly proportional to the applied force and takes place in the direction in which the force acts.
> $$F = \frac{dp}{dt} = m \cdot a$$

> [!TIP]
> **Third Law (Action & Reaction):** To every action there is always an equal and opposite reaction ($F_{12} = -F_{21}$).
