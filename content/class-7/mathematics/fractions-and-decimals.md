# Fractions and Decimals

## 1. Introduction
A fraction represents a part of a whole or any number of equal parts.

* **Proper Fraction:** Numerator is less than denominator ($\frac{3}{4}, \frac{5}{7}$).
* **Improper Fraction:** Numerator is greater than or equal to denominator ($\frac{7}{4}, \frac{9}{5}$).
* **Mixed Fraction:** Combination of whole number and proper fraction ($1\frac{3}{4}$).

## 2. Operations with Fractions

### Multiplication:
$$\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \times c}{b \times d}$$

### Division (Reciprocal Rule):
$$\frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c}$$

> [!NOTE]
> To convert a fraction to a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator. For example: $3/4 = 0.75$.
