# Basic Arithmetic Operations & BODMAS Rule

## 1. The Four Fundamental Operations
Arithmetic is the oldest and most elementary branch of mathematics used by everyone from daily transactions to scientific modeling:
* **Addition (+)**: Combining sets of numbers.
* **Subtraction (-)**: Finding the difference between two quantities.
* **Multiplication (×)**: Repeated addition of equal groups.
* **Division (÷)**: Splitting a number into equal parts.

## 2. Order of Operations: BODMAS
When evaluating mathematical expressions with multiple operations, follow the strict BODMAS hierarchy:

1. **B** - Brackets (parentheses `()`, braces `{}`, square brackets `[]`)
2. **O** - Orders (powers, indices, roots)
3. **D** - Division (evaluated left to right with Multiplication)
4. **M** - Multiplication
5. **A** - Addition (evaluated left to right with Subtraction)
6. **S** - Subtraction

### Example Evaluation:
$$12 + 4 \times (6 - 2) \div 2$$

Step 1: Inside Brackets: $(6 - 2) = 4$  
Step 2: Multiplication & Division: $4 \times 4 \div 2 = 16 \div 2 = 8$  
Step 3: Addition: $12 + 8 = 20$  

> [!TIP]
> Always solve innermost brackets first, working your way outwards!
