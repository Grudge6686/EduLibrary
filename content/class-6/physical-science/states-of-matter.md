# States of Matter and Phase Changes

## 1. Classification of Matter
Matter exists predominantly in three fundamental macroscopic states: solid, liquid, and gas.

| Property | Solid | Liquid | Gas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shape** | Definite | Indefinite (takes shape of container) | Indefinite (fills entire volume) |
| **Volume** | Fixed | Fixed | Not fixed |
| **Intermolecular Space** | Very small | Moderate | Large |
| **Intermolecular Force** | Very strong | Intermediate | Negligible |

## 2. Kinetic Molecular Theory
Particles in a substance possess kinetic energy corresponding to their temperature. When thermal energy is supplied:
1. Average kinetic energy increases.
2. Intermolecular bonds are overcome.
3. A phase transition occurs (melting, vaporization, sublimation).
